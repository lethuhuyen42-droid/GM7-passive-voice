Quick reminder: the passive voice

The passive is be + past participle (V3/ed). The verb "be" changes with the tense.

Present simple
am / is / are + V3/ed
The room is cleaned every day.
Past simple
was / were + V3/ed
The room was cleaned yesterday.
Present perfect
has / have been + V3/ed
The room has been cleaned.
